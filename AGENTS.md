# Photography privacy requirement

Ray explicitly requested: do not publish his face. Do not reintroduce personal headshots, graduation portraits, personal face avatars, withdrawn photography files, or photos/videos with visible people whose identity is uncertain. Preserve originals privately outside this repository.

Only privacy-reviewed gallery assets in public/photography-reviewed may be published. Every file must match src/data/approvedPhotographyMedia.json. A new/changed photo requires visual inspection of its full viewer image and preview; a video requires checking its entire duration. Automated face detection supplements review and does not establish identity or guarantee absence.

Run npm run check:photography-privacy before building or publishing photography changes. Do not restore public/photography, public/profile.jpeg, public/graduation cover.png or src/assets/profile*.png from old branches/history. Use the existing RX monogram for personal imagery.
